## IDR TEST BED

### Membrane Filtration for Companies

<!-- #### Summary -->

**The IDR Test Bed is a pilot-scale facility open to companies looking to test, develop, and optimize advanced wastewater treatment technologies using membrane filtration systems.**

![My Image](/img/img-testbed.webp)

<!-- #### Explanation -->

With a commitment to sharing knowledge and fostering innovation in the sector, IDR offers its advanced test plant to support tertiary wastewater treatment experiments and developments.

This facility presents a unique opportunity for companies in the environmental, industrial, technological, or research sectors that are interested in testing, validating, or enhancing membrane-based wastewater treatment systems.

Thanks to its advanced and adaptable infrastructure, the plant is a strategic tool for technological innovation and development. It allows companies to work with real-world, efficient, and sustainable treatment solutions.

The operational environment is ideal for developing pilot projects and conducting customized trials to evaluate filtered water quality and assess the processes' ability to meet emerging environmental or regulatory requirements.

### FACILITIES

#### First Stage

Physicochemical treatment system including:

- Two decanters (5 m³ and 3 m³)
- Three 1 m³ tanks for pH adjustment and reagent dosing
- Specific pH control dosing systems
- Six pumps for water recirculation
- One sand or glass filter for pre-filtration

#### Second Stage

Modular membrane filtration system incorporating:

- Ultrafiltration
- Nanofiltration
- Reverse osmosis technologies

#### Complementary Equipment

- Eight pumps for filtration and CIP (Clean-in-Place) operations
- Five auxiliary tanks
- Automated control system

### CONDITIONS

The facility can be booked for a minimum period of two weeks, with the possibility of extension. At the end of the usage period, a complete cleaning and conditioning of the installation is carried out.

Included services:

- Dedicated technical staff
- Power supply
- Basic water quality analyses
- Fully equipped office space
- Final report with results

Not included: membrane filters, chemical products, or replacement parts.
